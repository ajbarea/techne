# Lessons every surface shares

Decided lessons that made the work better. Read this and your surface's file before starting. To add or retire one, follow `/techne:lessons` or the rules at the end of this file.

### like-x-means-care: "Like X" means X's level of care

- **Rule:** Read an analogy as a quality bar, state that reading in one line before starting, and borrow X's names, characters or motifs only when the work is about X.
- **Why:** A literal read once put one project's characters on every unrelated talk.
- **Since:** 2026-11-05
- **Check:** guidance: an analogy's meaning cannot be computed.

### show-before-shipping: Show a change of look before shipping it

- **Rule:** When the ask is about a look, show before and after at 1920 wide and on a phone before shipping, and mock two or three directions on the same pages when asked for a look; the owner picks.
- **Why:** A look shipped unseen cost a full reversal across every deck.
- **Since:** 2026-11-08
- **Check:** guidance: taste is the owner's call.

### match-mood-first: A colour request names a mood

- **Rule:** A colour or theme request names a mood, so match how the subject really looks before adding saturation or volume.
- **Why:** Full-bleed orange title bars were rejected; a calm warm paper with orange as a small accent was approved.
- **Since:** 2026-11-09
- **Check:** guidance: fit to a subject cannot be computed.

### whole-surface: Read the whole rendered surface

- **Rule:** Before calling rendered work done, read all of it, especially what you did not touch, at 1920 wide and on a phone.
- **Why:** A polished figure pass once shipped beside raw table pipes in the page's main text.
- **Since:** 2026-06-07
- **Check:** guidance: a script captures the pages, a person reads them.

### rendered-not-present: A container is not the thing it holds

- **Rule:** Confirm a populated, visible child before saying an icon, link or button is there.
- **Why:** Themed slots render empty until configured, and a class-name match reads as present.
- **Since:** 2026-06-02
- **Check:** guidance: what counts as populated differs per element.

### describe-the-thing: Copy describes the thing

- **Rule:** User-facing copy presents the content; provenance such as "compiled from", "generated by" or "auto-updates" goes in a code comment or a quiet methods note.
- **Why:** Pipeline narration reads as machine-made and asserts what the data may not back.
- **Since:** 2026-06-09
- **Check:** guidance: such phrases are sometimes the true subject of the page.

### own-identity: Not the default look

- **Rule:** Each piece of work looks like itself, never the model's default (cream, navy, clay accent, rows of rounded cards).
- **Why:** A default look makes the work indistinguishable from everyone else's.
- **Since:** 2026-11-04
- **Check:** guidance: taste is the owner's call; `graphe:slides` reviews a deck that kept the starter's colours.

### concept-not-count: Name a group, do not count it

- **Rule:** In prose spanning several repositories or artifacts, say the concept and let the config resolve membership, never a hard-coded number or list of names.
- **Why:** Counts and lists go stale when the group changes.
- **Since:** 2026-05-20
- **Check:** guidance: a count is sometimes the fact being stated.
