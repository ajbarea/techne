# Driving Google Vids from Playwright

Verified in live sessions. Where a step records one session's observation, it says so.

1. **Open** `docs.google.com/videos/?authuser=<personal email>`; with several Google accounts signed in, `authuser` picks the right one (one session got a 403 page without it), but it does not prove which account opened: confirm the name in the account menu. The user signs in first.
2. **New video:** Blank video. Set the canvas with the toolbar's Video size combobox: Landscape 16:9, Portrait 9:16 (a Short) or Square 1:1.
3. **AI clip:** right rail **AI Video** (accessible name `Generate an AI video clip`) > **Create**. The **Avatar** slot (next to Ingredients) opens the Avatars dialog: pick **Me** (Personal avatar, the user's digital twin) > **Select**. That inserts a real `Me` chip (`prompt-chip-wrapper`) at the start of the prompt.
4. **Prompt:** click the box, Ctrl+End, Shift+Enter (a new line after the chip), then type with real key presses. Never `fill()`: it replaces the box and deletes the chip. The newline lands a beat late, so the first letters can split off onto their own text node (waiting 500 ms after Shift+Enter prevents it): if they do, put the caret at the start of the second fragment and press Backspace, then confirm the box reads `Me` then your first word. Start the text with a space so the prompt does not read `@MeStands`.
5. **Clothes, always:** describe the outfit in words in every clip, or the twin renders shirtless. A clothing image as an ingredient also works.
6. **Settings:** the button under the prompt (`Generation settings: Omni, 720p, Landscape, 10 seconds`) opens model, resolution (720p or 1080p), aspect ratio radios (Landscape, Portrait) and a duration slider (arrow keys move 1 s, the cap is 15 s since Omni 1.2; read its value from `aria-valuetext`, since `aria-valuenow` is empty). Set orientation here, not in the prompt.
7. **Quotes:** anything in double quotes is spoken; on-screen text goes in single quotes.
8. **Continuity:** Extend cannot re-add the avatar. For the next clip, use Create with the Me chip and add a screenshot of the last clip as an **Ingredient** (up to 3 images).
9. Refs in the accessibility snapshot change on every render; locate by role and name (`getByRole('radio', { name: 'Portrait' })`, `getByRole('button', { name: '720p', description: 'Resolution' })`).
10. **The prompt box changes name:** with ingredients attached it reads "Specify how you want to use the ingredients…", and it collapses after a generation (press its Expand button). Locate it as the panel's last textbox, not by label.
11. **Next clip:** Create, Ctrl+A and Backspace in the box, **Remove avatar**, then **Add** > **Avatar** > **Me** > **Select** for a fresh chip (with an image ingredient attached, the Avatar slot moves into the **Add avatar or ingredients** menu). Wait about half a second after Shift+Enter and the first-letter split does not happen.
12. **Continuity still:** download the clip (open its video URL in a new tab, which triggers a download), take the last frame with ffmpeg, crop off any baked caption, and upload it through **Add** > **Upload** (a Drive picker: its Upload tab, then Browse).
13. **Check a clip without playing it:** sample frames into a contact sheet with ffmpeg and transcribe the audio locally (faster-whisper `base.en`) to confirm the spoken lines word for word.
14. **Insert:** the first clip goes in with Insert; later clips offer **Insert in new scene**. Scenes can be dragged to reorder in the timeline.
15. **Music:** right rail **Music** opens "Generate a song" (Lyria 3.5): describe genre and mood and paste lyrics; options are Duration (30s or Full) and an Instrumental toggle. Use it before any outside music tool.
16. **Mix:** select the song track, open **Sound**: set a fixed Volume with ducking Off (see step 20), and a 2 s fade out. To change a ducking radio, clicking its label text worked where clicking the radio did not. Then File > Download > MP4 and transcribe the mix: every spoken line must come through word for word.
17. **Hearing it:** the Playwright MCP browser starts with `--mute-audio`, so verify by transcript and let the user listen in their own browser or from the exported MP4.
18. **Retakes:** generate each clip twice and keep the better one; a contact sheet at 2 fps catches the slop a transcript misses (props that never fully exist, a shot wider than asked, logo or lettering drift such as a jersey's two initials rendering as 'AI').
19. **Generated clips are temporary:** the AI panel's takes live in temp storage and are gone after a page reload; only clips inserted into the timeline persist. Insert (or download) every take worth keeping before leaving the page. After a reload the generation settings reset too (720p, 10 s), so set them again.
20. **Ducking pitfall:** marking clips **Narrative** lowers every other track for the whole time each clip plays, not only while someone speaks; with every clip narrative, the song stays buried whatever its volume. Usually leave ducking Off and set the song's fixed volume (80% put the song about 5 dB under the AI clips' dialogue, -25 vs -20 dB mean, with every line still transcribed).
21. **Export only what is saved:** wait for **Saved to Drive** before File > Download > MP4, or the export can be the previous version (two exports came out byte-identical after an unsaved change).
22. **Replacing a scene:** right-click the scene > Delete, then Insert > Upload (or File menu, or right-click > upload from computer) a downloaded take; uploading can add the clip twice, so check the timeline and delete the duplicate. Scenes reorder by drag, after **Zoom to fit timeline** (the magnifier with a square).
23. **Transitions:** the double-circle icon between scenes adds a transition with a length; it eats into each clip's generated time, so a fade can cut audio. Do not drag a clip past its generated length (it fills with blank frames).
24. **More tools:** text, images, shapes and animations insert from the right rail; names can be spoken without anyone shown (first-person POV puts the viewer in a person's place).
25. **Uploading from a script:** after `fileChooser.setFiles()` inside `browser_run_code`, cancel the MCP's own pending file chooser (`browser_file_upload` with no paths). Answering it again uploads the file a second time.
26. **Volume is not linear:** on one clip, 60% measured 16 dB quieter than 100%, and 85% about 6 dB quieter. Set a level, export, measure `mean_volume` with ffmpeg, and adjust.
27. **Song lyrics as karaoke:** Vids captions transcribe speech, not singing. For on-screen lyrics, mute every clip for one export so only the song plays, take word times from a faster-whisper `word_timestamps` pass over it (the lyrics as `initial_prompt`), unmute, and draw the lyrics over the final export locally (Pillow frames piped to ffmpeg, words filling in as sung). A lyric scene of its own can also be rendered and uploaded into Vids.
28. **Trimming an audio track:** drag its right edge (the cursor turns to `ew-resize`) so it stops where the next track starts.
29. **Memory:** reload the Vids tab every few clips; one long session grew the tab to 4 GB. Under WSL, `memory=` in `.wslconfig` caps what the browser can use.
30. **The Shorts overlay:** on a phone, the channel row, title, AI badge and buttons cover roughly the bottom 30% of the frame, measured from a phone screenshot of an uploaded Short. Keep text drawn on a Short above about 600 px from the bottom of a 1920 px frame; guides that quote 288 to 300 px put it under the title.

## Animating a still (the Animate tab)

Verified in one session on 2026-11-18. The method for an idle loop is in [idle-loop.md](idle-loop.md).

31. **Tabs:** the AI Video panel has Create, Edit, Animate and Extend. **Animate** takes one start image (**Add image**) and a prompt. It has no last-frame slot, so a loop is cut from inside the take.
32. **Settings:** the model menu offers only Omni. Resolution is 720p or 1080p, aspect is Landscape or Portrait (no square, even on a Square canvas) and the duration slider showed 3 to 15 s on 2026-11-18 ([the help page](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/16143507) says 3 to 10 s for Animate). Close the settings popover before clicking **Add image**: while open, it intercepts the click.
33. **Upload through the Drive picker:** **Add image** opens a picker in an iframe whose URL contains `/picker/`. Its **Upload** tab has no `input[type=file]` until **Browse** is clicked. Register `page.waitForEvent('filechooser')`, click Browse, `setFiles()`, then cancel the MCP's own pending chooser (step 25).
34. **Takes:** one Generate makes one take, in one to two minutes for 6 s at 1080p. A take is 1920x1080 (or 1080x1920) H.264 at 24 fps with an exact frame count (6 s = 144 frames) and an AAC track even when the prompt says no music. A take can fail with "Something went wrong"; its **Try again** button re-queues it.
35. **Recreate** restores that take's image, prompt and settings, aspect included. With several takes in the panel, the first match is the oldest, so read the settings label before pressing Generate.
36. **Download a raw take:** read the panel video's `currentSrc` (`contribution-rt.usercontent.google.com`), open it in a new page, wait for the `download` event and `download.saveAs()` into a folder the MCP allows (it refuses others, such as a scratchpad). `require()` is not available inside `browser_run_code`. Download each take as soon as it lands: the panel is temporary, and the MCP browser lost its tab twice in that session.
37. **Timeline export of a take:** File > Download re-encodes. On a Square canvas, a 16:9 take comes out 1080x1080 at 30 fps, letterboxed on white bars (picture rows 236 to 844). Use the raw take (step 36) for scoring and loop cuts.
38. **After a reload,** a start dialog (Landscape, Portrait, Square and entry tiles) can cover the editor; close it or pick **Blank vid** first.
